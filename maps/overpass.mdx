Overpass features extensive CT side utilities including smokes, mollies, and wallbangs, with T side wallbang positions.

## CT Side Line-ups

The CT side configuration provides comprehensive defensive utilities.

### Primary Wheel (Tab 0)
- **Position 1**: Wallbang 1
- **Position 2**: Molly 2
- **Position 8**: Wallbang 3

### Secondary Wheel (Tab 1)
- **Position 1**: CT Smoke 2
- **Position 2**: CT Smoke 3
- **Position 3**: CT Smoke 5
- **Position 5**: Molly 1
- **Position 7**: CT Smoke 4
- **Position 8**: CT Smoke 1

### Tertiary Wheel (Tab 2)
- **Position 1**: Wallbang 1
- **Position 2**: Molly 2
- **Position 8**: Wallbang 3

<Info>
  Overpass CT side features 5 smoke positions, 2 molly positions, and 3 wallbang spots for comprehensive defensive coverage.
</Info>

## T Side Line-ups

The T side configuration focuses on wallbang positions.

### Primary Wheel (Tab 0)
- **Position 1**: More options (not supported)
- **Position 3**: Scroll for more utilities
- **Position 7**: Scroll for more utilities

### Secondary Wheel (Tab 1)
- **Position 5**: Wallbang 2
- **Position 6**: Wallbang 4

### Tertiary Wheel (Tab 2)
- Scroll navigation for extended utilities

<Tip>
  The wallbang positions on both sides are particularly valuable on Overpass due to the map's many wallbangable surfaces through connector and bathrooms.
</Tip>
