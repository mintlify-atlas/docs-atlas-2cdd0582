Mirage features the most extensive utility configurations in the CSAFAP package, with comprehensive T side options including connector, standard, and top connector smokes, plus CT side wallbangs.

## CT Side Line-ups

The CT side configuration includes smokes and wallbang positions.

### Primary Wheel (Tab 0)
- **Position 1**: More options (not supported)
- **Position 3**: Scroll for more utilities
- **Position 7**: Scroll for more utilities

### Secondary Wheel (Tab 1)
- **Position 1**: Wallbang 1
- **Position 2**: Silent (utility)
- **Position 4**: CT Smoke 1
- **Position 7**: Wallbang 2

### Tertiary Wheel (Tab 2)
- Scroll navigation for extended utilities

<Info>
  Mirage CT side includes wallbang positions for common pre-fire spots and defensive smoke options.
</Info>

## T Side Line-ups

The T side configuration is the most comprehensive in the package, with three full smoke sets.

### Primary Wheel (Tab 0) - Connector Smokes
- **Position 1**: Connector Smoke 4
- **Position 2**: Connector Smoke 3
- **Position 3**: Connector Smoke 1
- **Position 5**: Scroll for more utilities
- **Position 7**: Connector Smoke 2
- **Position 8**: Connector Smoke 5

### Secondary Wheel (Tab 1) - Standard Smokes
- **Position 1**: Smoke 4
- **Position 2**: Smoke 3
- **Position 3**: Smoke 1
- **Position 5**: 5 A-Site Smokes (full execute)
- **Position 7**: Smoke 2
- **Position 8**: Smoke 5

### Tertiary Wheel (Tab 2) - Top Connector Smokes
- **Position 1**: Top Connector Smoke 4
- **Position 2**: Top Connector Smoke 3
- **Position 3**: Top Connector Smoke 1
- **Position 5**: Scroll for more utilities
- **Position 7**: Top Connector Smoke 2
- **Position 8**: Top Connector Smoke 5

<Tip>
  The "5 A-Site Smokes" option on the secondary wheel executes a full A-site take with all necessary smokes in sequence.
</Tip>

## Configuration Files

Source files: `~/workspace/source/csafap/maps/mirage/`
- `mirage_CT_labels.cfg` - CT side radio wheel labels
- `mirage_T_labels.cfg` - T side radio wheel labels
- `mirage_CT_cmd.cfg` - CT side command bindings
- `mirage_T_cmd.cfg` - T side command bindings

## Map-Specific Tips

<Tip>
  Mirage offers three distinct smoke sets for T side:
  - **Connector smokes**: For mid control and B splits
  - **Standard smokes**: General-purpose utilities
  - **Top connector smokes**: Alternative mid-control options from different positions
</Tip>
