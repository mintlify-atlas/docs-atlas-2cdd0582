Nuke features CT defensive smokes and wallbangs, with minimal T side utility configurations.

## CT Side Line-ups

The CT side configuration provides defensive smokes and a wallbang position.

### Primary Wheel (Tab 0)
- **Position 1**: More options (not supported)
- **Position 3**: Scroll for more utilities
- **Position 7**: Scroll for more utilities

### Secondary Wheel (Tab 1)
- **Position 1**: Smoke 3
- **Position 2**: Smoke 4
- **Position 3**: Smoke 5
- **Position 7**: Wallbang 1

### Tertiary Wheel (Tab 2)
- Scroll navigation for extended utilities

<Info>
  Nuke CT side includes 3 smoke positions and a wallbang, focused on defensive utility for the unique vertical map layout.
</Info>

## T Side Line-ups

The T side configuration includes 2 smoke positions.

### Primary Wheel (Tab 0)
- **Position 1**: More options (not supported)
- **Position 3**: Scroll for more utilities
- **Position 7**: Scroll for more utilities

### Secondary Wheel (Tab 1)
- **Position 1**: Smoke 1
- **Position 8**: Smoke 2

### Tertiary Wheel (Tab 2)
- Scroll navigation for extended utilities

<Tip>
  Nuke's vertical gameplay makes smoke line-ups particularly important for upper and lower site executes. The 2 T side smokes cover essential site approaches.
</Tip>
