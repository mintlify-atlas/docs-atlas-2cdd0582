Train features basic CT defensive smokes and extensive T side smoke configurations with 15 different positions.

## CT Side Line-ups

The CT side configuration provides 3 defensive smoke positions.

### Primary Wheel (Tab 0)
- **Position 1**: More options (not supported)
- **Position 3**: Scroll for more utilities
- **Position 7**: Scroll for more utilities

### Secondary Wheel (Tab 1)
- **Position 2**: CT Smoke 2
- **Position 3**: CT Smoke 1
- **Position 4**: CT Smoke 3

### Tertiary Wheel (Tab 2)
- Scroll navigation for extended utilities

<Info>
  Train CT side provides 3 essential defensive smoke positions for holding key areas of the map.
</Info>
